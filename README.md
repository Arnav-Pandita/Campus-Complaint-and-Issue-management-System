# Campus-Complaint-and-Issue-management-System
A web-based campus complaint management system with anonymous reporting, department-wise administration, and accountability tracking.

## Project Overview

The Campus Complaint & Issue Management System is a web-based application designed to provide a centralized platform for students to report campus-related issues, track complaint progress, and view complaints submitted by other students.

The system provides separate interfaces for students, department administrators, and a Super Admin to support organized complaint handling, accountability, and transparency.

## Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for submitting and tracking campus complaints.
- Improve transparency through a public complaint board.
- Support anonymous complaint submission with private tracking.
- Enable department-wise complaint management.
- Improve accountability through priority reasons, status history, and dashboards.

## Key Features

- Student registration and authentication.
- Complaint submission with category and department selection.
- Visible and anonymous complaint options.
- Image attachments with privacy warnings.
- Public complaint board and similar-complaint suggestions.
- Department-wise complaint management.
- Priority assignment with mandatory reasons.
- Complaint status tracking and resolution history.
- Reopening requests for unresolved issues.
- In-app notifications.
- Accountability dashboards for department administrators and the Super Admin.

## User Roles

1. **Student:** Submits, views, and tracks complaints.
2. **Department Admin:** Manages complaints assigned to their department.
3. **Super Admin:** Oversees all departments, manages department administrators, and reassigns complaints when required.

## Departments in Scope

- Maintenance & Facilities
- IT & Network
- Academic Administration
- Library
- Canteen & Food Services

An "Other" option is available for complaints requiring assignment by the Super Admin. Hostel administration is excluded from the current project scope.
